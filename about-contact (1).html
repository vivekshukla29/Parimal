Our Story

A craft passed down
through generations.

Parimal Industries was born from a simple yet sacred belief — that fragrance is not merely a scent, but a memory, a prayer, a presence. From our atelier in Araul, Kanpur, we have devoted ourselves to one pursuit alone: capturing the soul of India in every wisp of agarbatti, every drop of attar, every ember of dhoop.

What began as a small workshop has grown into a trusted name across Indian households and temples — yet the craft remains unchanged. Every stick is hand-rolled. Every blend is hand-mixed. Every product carries with it the quiet patience of artisans who measure success not in volume, but in the depth of the fragrance they leave behind.

Pure Intent
No shortcuts. No synthetics.
Patient Craft
Slow, deliberate, devoted.
Trust Earned
10,000+ happy families.

Today, Parimal stands as a humble custodian of this timeless tradition — bringing to your home and altar the same purity our ancestors held sacred. We do not chase trends. We honour what has always been true.

— Parimal
The Founder Crafting fragrance since 2015
